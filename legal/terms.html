Who can use Hits

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must link to your account before you can be found by other players or arrange anything. They approve each meetup individually.
  • One account per person. Your date of birth must be true. Saying you are older or younger than you are is the one thing here that gets an account removed without warning.

What Hits does, and what it does not do

Hits introduces players. It does not supervise them. We do not run background checks, we do not verify identity beyond a phone number, and nobody from Hits is at the court. A meetup arranged through Hits is between you and the other player, and if you are under 18, your parent or guardian.

We verify a UTR rating when you ask us to, by a person checking your public UTR profile. That confirms a number. It confirms nothing else about who someone is.

Meeting people safely

  • Play at public courts, in daylight, where other people are around.
  • If you are under 18, tell your parent or guardian where you are going — and they have already approved the specific meetup, so they know.
  • You never have to share your phone number. If you do, it is for one hit and you can take it back.
  • If something feels wrong, leave. Then report it in the app.

Rules

Do not use Hits to:

  • Lie about your age, your identity or your rating.
  • Contact someone who has blocked you, or make an account to get around a block.
  • Send anything sexual, threatening, hateful or harassing — to anyone, ever, and especially not to a minor.
  • Advertise, sell, recruit or solicit. Hits is not a marketplace and coaching for money is not what it is for.
  • Collect other players' information, or use Hits through a bot or a scraper.

We can suspend or remove an account that breaks these, and we do not have to warn you first if someone's safety is involved.

Reporting

Every profile and every conversation has a report button. A person reads every report, and reports involving a minor go to the front of the queue. We aim to respond the same day. See Support. If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first — we are not an emergency service.
