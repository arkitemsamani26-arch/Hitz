Hits is used by people under 18. That changes what we collect and who can see it, so this policy is specific rather than general. If something here does not match what the app actually does, the app is wrong and we want to know: [support@yourdomain].

What we collect

Phone numberHow you sign in. We send a one-time code by SMS. Stored separately from your public profile.
First name and last initialShown to other players. We do not ask for your full surname.
Date of birthDecides whether you are treated as an under-18 or an adult. Collected once and not editable afterwards.
PhotoOptional. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian approves it before anyone else sees it, and can remove it at any time.
LocationTwo forms. A rounded position, snapped to roughly a two-kilometre grid, is what other players' distances are calculated from. The more precise position your device reported is held on our server, is never shown to another player, and is never sent back to any app.
Tennis levelYour UTR or self-reported level. If you ask us to verify it, the UTR profile link and the name on it, so a person can check the two match.
AvailabilityWhich mornings, afternoons and evenings you can usually play.
MessagesWhat you write to the other player about a specific hit.
Your guardian's detailsIf you are under 18: your parent or guardian's email address and phone number, so we can reach them for approvals. This is personal information about someone else, and we treat it that way.
Device push tokenAn anonymous identifier so we can notify you about a request, an approval or tomorrow's hit. Nothing else.

We do not collect your exact address, your contacts, your photo library, your microphone, or anything about what you do in other apps. There is no advertising in Hits, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Who can see what

Under-18s and adults never see each other. This is not a setting. It is enforced by the database, so a fault in the app cannot reveal an under-18 to an adult or the other way round.

  • Other players see your first name and last initial, your photo if you have one, your level, your approximate distance from them, and how often you reply.
  • Your phone number is hidden by default. You can share it for one confirmed hit, one at a time, and take it back afterwards.
  • Your parent or guardian, if you are under 18, sees who you are arranging to meet, where and when, the messages in that conversation, and your photo. They approve or refuse each meetup.
  • Nobody sees your date of birth, your precise location, or your phone number unless you shared it for a hit.

If you are under 18

You cannot be found by other players, and cannot arrange a hit, until a parent or guardian has linked to your account and confirmed it is them. Every meetup then needs their approval separately — approving one does not approve the next. They can remove your photo, block another player on your behalf, or end the link at any time, and ending it stops you arranging anything new.

Hits is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we never will. We use these services to run Hits, and they only handle what they need to:

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • [SMS provider] — sends your sign-in code.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Universal Tennis (UTR) — only if you choose to link or verify your UTR rating.

We will also hand over information if the law requires it, or if we believe someone is in danger.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: You → Delete my account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Your profile, photo, phone number, location, messages and hit history are removed, any hit that has not yet happened is cancelled and the other player is told it is off, and your login is deleted.

One thing survives. If another player has reported you, that report stays. It no longer carries your name, your photo or anything you wrote — only the fact that it was made and who it was about. We keep it because deleting your account would otherwise be the fastest way to erase a safety report, which is the opposite of what a report is for.

We also keep a record that an account existed and was deleted. It contains no personal information about you.

How long we keep things

  • Your profile and messages: until you delete your account.
  • Completed hits: kept while your account exists, so your history and response rate make sense.
  • Safety reports: [retention period, e.g. three years], as described above.
  • Sign-in codes: minutes.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest. The rules about who can read what are enforced in the database rather than in the app, and they are covered by an automated test suite that runs on every change.

Changes
