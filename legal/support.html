Parents and guardians

You do not need the app to be involved. The link you were sent by text or email opens a page showing who your child is arranging to meet, where and when, and lets you approve or refuse it. Nothing happens without you.

  • To end the link — open the page and choose End link, or email us. Your child can no longer arrange anything new.
  • To remove your child's photo — from the same page, at any time.
  • To block someone on your child's behalf — from their profile on that page.
  • To delete your child's account — email us and we will do it, or they can do it themselves under You → Delete my account.

Reporting someone

Every profile and every conversation has a report button. Reports involving someone under 18 go to the front of the queue. You will hear back from us once a person has looked at it. Reports are confidential — we never tell the person who reported them.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: You → Delete my account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. What is removed and what is kept is set out in the privacy policy.

Why can't I see any adults / any juniors?

That is deliberate. Under-18s and adults cannot see each other in Hits at all.

Why is my hit still waiting?

If either player is under 18, their parent or guardian has to approve that specific meetup before it is confirmed. Until then it is a proposal.

How do I get the verified badge?

Send us your UTR rating and a link to your UTR profile from You → Get verified. A person checks the two match. Usually within a day.

Can I use Hits without sharing my location?

Not usefully — distance is how players are matched. What other players see is a rounded position, never where you actually are.

Something is wrong with the app

Email us with what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps more than anything else you can send.
